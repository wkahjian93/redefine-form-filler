Step 1

Choose a team form or add a new one

Pick a form your team already uses, or upload a new blank PDF or photo. For a new form, the app finds every box and AI works out what goes in each one. Once it's filled and exported, it's saved here for every agent.

Step 2

Add customer details

Drop in anything that holds the details: photos or screenshots, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel or CSV. An Excel sheet with one customer per row becomes one customer per row.

Snap the IC, a policy page or handwritten notes.

    Read with AI

    AI reads each file and fills the fields of the form you picked in step 1. Nothing is guessed: details that aren't in the files are left blank and listed for you to check. Client details go through the REDEFINE team server to OpenAI. REDEFINE does not save them. Remove details the form doesn't need, such as medical records, before uploading.

    No AI? Use the Excel template

    Columns named like the template are filled directly, without AI.
